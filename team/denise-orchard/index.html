Meet your counselor

Denise Orchard

MSW, LISW-S · Licensed Independent Social Worker

Denise Orchard, MSW, LISW-S, is a licensed independent social worker with more than 30 years of experience working with children, adults, and families in clinical and child welfare settings. She provides virtual counseling and has experience supporting individuals experiencing grief, depression, anxiety, and other life challenges through individualized counseling support.

Denise also brings extensive professional experience in foster care and adoption, including leadership and direct practice with children and families. Her clinical and child welfare background provides a strong understanding of the impact of trauma, family transitions, and significant life changes on individuals and families.

Denise is committed to creating a supportive, collaborative counseling environment that helps clients build on their strengths, develop coping strategies, and work toward meaningful goals.
