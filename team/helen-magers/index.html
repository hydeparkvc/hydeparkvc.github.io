Meet your counselor

Helen Magers

LPCC · Licensed Professional Clinical Counselor

Helen brings more than 20 years of experience helping adults, adolescents, and couples navigate the ups and downs of life. She works with clients experiencing anxiety, depression, grief and loss, PTSD, relationship challenges, and other difficult seasons of life.

Whether you are feeling overwhelmed, trying to make sense of something painful that has happened, adjusting to a major life change, or simply wanting to feel more connected in your relationships, Helen offers a warm and supportive place to work through it. She believes counseling is not about having all the answers. It is about having someone alongside you who will listen, understand, and help you find a path forward.

Helen takes a compassionate, thoughtful approach and meets each client where they are. She works collaboratively with clients to better understand what they are going through, build on their strengths, and make changes that feel meaningful and achievable.

Specialties and approach

Anxiety · Depression · Grief · Loss · PTSD · Communication · Couples · EMDR

Helen is trained in EMDR and brings more than two decades of experience and genuine care to her work.
