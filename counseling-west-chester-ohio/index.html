Secure virtual counseling in West Chester, Ohio

Online counseling for adults in West Chester

If stress, low mood, grief, or relationship changes have been taking up too much space, you deserve a place where you can talk openly and feel heard. Reaching out can be a first step toward understanding what you need.

Support that meets you where you are

Hyde Park Virtual Counseling offers individualized outpatient counseling for adults working through anxiety and stress, depression, grief and loss, trauma-related concerns, relationship challenges, and life transitions. You and your counselor can talk about what has been difficult and identify goals that feel meaningful to you.

Virtual counseling across Ohio

Care is provided exclusively through secure telehealth; Hyde Park Virtual Counseling does not have an in-person office in West Chester. Sessions are available to clients who are physically located in Ohio. Contact us to ask about current counselor availability, insurance, fees, and next steps.
