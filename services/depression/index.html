Counseling for depression

When getting through the day feels harder than it used to.

Depression can look like sadness, but it can also feel like numbness, exhaustion, irritability, disconnection, or losing interest in the things that once mattered. You may be functioning on the outside while feeling depleted inside. You deserve support that meets you where you are.

You are not failing

Depression can affect sleep, concentration, relationships, motivation, and your sense of hope. Small tasks may feel overwhelming, and you may be judging yourself for not being able to simply push through. Counseling offers a place to be honest about what is happening without having to perform or have all the answers.

Finding a way forward

Your counselor can help you understand the patterns keeping you stuck and identify manageable steps toward relief. Sessions may include space to process what you are carrying, practical tools for daily life, and evidence-based approaches such as CBT, IPT-D, or DBT skills when they fit your goals.

  • Reconnect with meaning, energy, and supportive relationships
  • Build routines that feel realistic rather than overwhelming
  • Develop skills for difficult thoughts and emotions
  • Make changes at a pace that respects your experience
