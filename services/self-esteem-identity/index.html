Counseling for self-esteem and identity

You deserve to feel at home in your own life.

You may be constantly comparing yourself, second-guessing your choices, putting everyone else first, or feeling disconnected from who you are. Maybe you are exploring identity, values, relationships, or a major change. Counseling can offer a steady place to understand yourself with more compassion.

When self-doubt becomes exhausting

Low self-esteem can affect how you speak to yourself, what you tolerate in relationships, and whether you let yourself pursue what matters. You may know what you want but feel afraid to take up space. These patterns often developed for understandable reasons, and they can be explored without blame.

Building a more grounded sense of self

Your counselor will help you notice the beliefs and experiences shaping your self-image, identify your values, and practice choices that feel more authentic. The work may include boundaries, self-compassion, communication, confidence, and making room for the parts of yourself you have learned to hide.

  • Challenge harsh self-judgment and people-pleasing patterns
  • Build boundaries and trust in your own decisions
  • Explore identity and values with curiosity
  • Move toward relationships and goals that fit who you are
