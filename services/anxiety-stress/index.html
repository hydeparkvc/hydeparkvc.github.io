Counseling for anxiety and stress

You do not have to keep carrying the worry alone.

Anxiety can make even ordinary moments feel exhausting. You may be overthinking conversations, bracing for something to go wrong, avoiding situations you used to enjoy, or feeling tense in your body all day. Support can help you understand what is happening and find steadier ground.

When anxiety is taking up too much space

You might be dealing with panic, social anxiety, persistent worry, work stress, sleep problems, irritability, or a feeling that your mind will not slow down. Sometimes anxiety shows up as perfectionism or difficulty making decisions. You do not need to wait until it becomes unmanageable to ask for help.

How counseling can help

Your counselor will listen without judgment and work with you to understand your patterns, triggers, needs, and goals. Together, you can practice practical skills for calming your nervous system, challenging unhelpful thought loops, setting boundaries, and moving toward the parts of life anxiety has made smaller.

  • Build tools for panic, worry, and overwhelm
  • Develop more confidence in daily decisions and relationships
  • Learn ways to respond to stress with greater flexibility
  • Create a plan that fits your life and pace
